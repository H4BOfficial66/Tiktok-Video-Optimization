How It Works & Fixing TikTok Public Visibility

  • 1
    Exact Video Duration & Zero Shadowban (1x Safe Mode)

    Older frame-inflation hacks inflated declared frames by adding dummy frames, turning a 13-second video into 1:20+ min with dead silence and frozen screens. TikTok's AI detects this freeze and restricts the video to creator-only (0 views). 1x Safe Mode preserves your exact video duration, aligns audio to the millisecond, and ensures your post is 100% public to everyone!

  • 2
    Fast-Start Layout (moov before mdat) & High Profile

    Relocates the moov atom before mdat for instant streaming on TikTok, patches avcC to High Profile (profile 100), cleans non-essential SEI NALUs, and updates btrt bitrates without altering your original pixels.

  • 3
    TikTok App Upload Settings

    In TikTok: Go to Profile > Settings > Data Saver (turn OFF). When uploading a post, tap More options > toggle "Allow high-quality uploads" to ON.
